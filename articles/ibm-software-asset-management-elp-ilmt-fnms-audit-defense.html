Licensing Metrics Deep Dive

Practitioner Notes

Memorize three examples: Intel bare metal, VMware sub-cap VM, POWER9 LPAR. They show you can calculate, not just define.

IBM metrics are not interchangeable. Each metric requires a different evidence chain, formula, validation step, and practitioner story.

MetricDefinitionFormula / Counting RuleWorked ExamplePitfall
PVUProcessor Value Unit for distributed processor capacity.Full Capacity = physical cores x PVU/core. Sub-Capacity = VM/LPAR vCPU or allocated cores x PVU/core, subject to ILMT.WebSphere AS ND on 2 x 24-core Intel host at 70 PVU/core = 3,360 PVU full capacity. MQ Advanced VM with 8 vCPU at 70 = 560 PVU if ILMT is healthy. Db2 on POWER9 4-core LPAR at 120 = 480 PVU.POWER processors carry higher PVU/core values than Intel. Without ILMT, IBM can default to full-capacity.
RVUResource Value Unit for managed things such as TB, endpoints, nodes, or devices.Apply product tier bands, then sum each tier.Spectrum Protect managing 150 TB: first 100 TB x 1.0 = 100 RVU, next 50 x 0.5 = 25 RVU, total 125 RVU.Never assume one RVU equals one device; the managed resource changes by product.
MVSManaged Virtual Server; each managed VM, LPAR, or node counts.Count managed virtual servers rather than physical hosts.QRadar managing 220 VMs consumes 220 MVS if entitlement is per managed server.A physical host with ten VMs can be ten MVS. For containers, confirm node vs pod counting in product terms.
AUAuthorized User; a named person authorized to access the program from any device.Unique authorized people, not concurrent sessions.Cognos with 250 named business users consumes 250 AU even if each uses laptop and mobile.Inactive AD accounts and service IDs can inflate counts; validate with application owner.
AUSIAuthorized User Single Install; named user per installation/server.User-server pairs, plus product minimums such as AU per PVU/core.The same 80 Db2 users on PROD and DR require 160 AUSI if both installations are in scope.Practitioners often count users once; IBM counts access per install.
FUL / ConcurrentFloating or concurrent pool based on peak simultaneous usage.Peak checked-out licenses or active sessions.ClearCase team of 200 with peak 40 users needs 40 FUL if evidence supports the peak.Great optimization lever, but needs license-server logs and defined peak window.
Install / ServerFlat entitlement per installation, server, protected VM, or agent.One unit per defined install target.50 protected VMs under an install metric consume 50 install licenses.Read the License Information document; install metrics vary by product bundle.
VPCVirtual Processor Core for Cloud Paks.vCPU allocated to container x product ratio; measured by ILS over rolling peak.CP4I component using 32 vCPU at 1:1 consumes 32 VPC. Aspera 4 vCPU at 0.25:1 consumes 1 VPC.Cloud Paks use IBM License Service, not ILMT. 90-day rolling peak still matters.
MSU / MIPSMainframe capacity units.Mainframe capacity by subsystem or contract basis.z/OS and CICS positions are handled with mainframe usage and contract evidence.Do not mix distributed PVU logic with mainframe MSU terms.

Sub-Capacity Licensing Rules

Practitioner Notes

Say: “Health check first, reconcile second. I do not use sub-capacity numbers unless the ILMT evidence is audit-defensible.”

Sub-capacity is a contractual concession. IBM lets the client license only the virtualized allocation only when the evidence proves continuous eligible tracking. If one requirement fails, full-capacity exposure becomes the defensible fallback.

  1. ILMT must be installed within 90 days of first eligible product deployment.
  2. ILMT must collect continuously, not only at quarter-end.
  3. Reports and snapshots must be retained for at least two years.
  4. The hypervisor must be recognized by IBM for sub-capacity.
  5. Quarterly reporting must be produced and internally retained.

Linux KVMYesRHEL/SUSE KVM supported; verify IBM support list for current releases.

Biggest IBM Audit Risk

Failing any sub-capacity criterion can turn a 560 PVU VM position into a 3,360 PVU full-capacity host position. That multiplication is why ILMT health is not an admin detail; it is the financial control.

Cloud Pak / ILS Model

Practitioner Notes

Most teams forget ratios. Say the ratio table out loud and mention ILS without being prompted.

Cloud Paks are OpenShift-oriented bundles licensed in Virtual Processor Cores. The entitlement lets the client run eligible bundled products, but consumption depends on the component ratio and rolling peak reported by IBM License Service.

Cloud Pak ComponentConsumption RatioExample
MQ Advanced1:14 vCPU running MQ Advanced consumes 4 VPC.
API Connect1:110 vCPU consumes 10 VPC.
App Connect Enterprise1:16 vCPU consumes 6 VPC.
DataPower Gateway1:18 vCPU consumes 8 VPC.
Aspera0.25:14 vCPU consumes 1 VPC.

Traditional PVU estates may be eligible for PVU-to-VPC trade-up using IBM-published conversion ratios. This is both a compliance topic and a commercial optimization topic: the right answer may be a conversion, not a pure true-up.

ILS vs ILMT

Cloud Paks use IBM License Service deployed as an operator on OpenShift. ILS reports VPC consumption per product with rolling peak behavior; ILMT remains central for traditional PVU sub-capacity.

Manual ELP Creation (Step-by-Step)

Practitioner Notes

Open with the equation, then prove defensibility: entitlement evidence, deployment evidence, metric math, assumptions, and audit trail.

An IBM ELP is the reconciled gap between entitlements owned and entitlements consumed, calculated per product, edition, metric, and version, factoring in upgrade/downgrade rights and S&S status.

Practitioner opener
An IBM ELP is the reconciled gap between entitlements owned and entitlements consumed, calculated per product, per metric, per version, factoring in version upgrade/downgrade rights and S&S status. The output drives both compliance defence and cost optimization.
Core ELP equation
Position = Entitlements Owned - Deployment Consumed\nPosition > 0 = GREEN surplus / optimization\nPosition = 0 = GREEN aligned\nPosition < 0 = RED exposure\nQuantity OK but version/S&S/sub-cap evidence weak = AMBER

Data Collection With and Without Tools

Practitioner Notes

Senior line: “If the data is incomplete, I produce a confidence-rated ELP, not a pretend-final ELP.”

With tools, the job is source validation. Without tools, the job is controlled reconstruction. In both cases the evidence chain must explain where each number came from, who provided it, when it was extracted, and what weakness remains.

Partial Data Handling

Do not guess silently. State the assumption, choose the defensible worst case if risk-bearing, assign an owner, and create a remediation action. A weak ELP can still be useful if its confidence boundaries are explicit.

Linked Licenses Matter

In FNMS, entitlement may sit in purchases while consumption sits in applications. If applications are not linked to the correct IBM license record, the ELP is structurally wrong even if inventory discovery is perfect.

Draft vs Final FNMS Output

FNMS output is draft when health checks, entitlement links, ARL/PURL currency, ILMT reconciliation, and analyst assumptions are not complete. It becomes final only when it is cross-validated and evidence-backed.

ILMT Deep Knowledge

Practitioner Notes

Absent ILMT Consequence

If ILMT is absent, late, unhealthy, or incomplete for sub-capacity PVU products, calculate the defensible fallback as full capacity and flag the exposure. Never present an unsupported sub-cap number as final.

Practitioner Notes

Say: “Procurement tells me what the client thinks they bought; Passport Advantage tells me what IBM will recognize.”

IBM entitlement analysis starts with Passport Advantage and ends with contractual rights: metric, quantity, version, S&S, site ID, legal entity, and special terms.

DocumentWhat It ProvesExtract These FieldsPractitioner Watchout

Upgrade rights usually require active S&S when the newer version became available. Downgrade rights can help with older deployments, but only within the same eligible product and metric. If S&S lapsed, the client keeps the right to use the version available at lapse date but not later versions, patches, or support.

When Portal Conflicts With Procurement

Use Passport Advantage as the official IBM entitlement ledger, then reconcile procurement records as supporting evidence. If procurement shows more, ask reseller/IBM to prove the PA record or identify a different site/legal entity.

Practitioner Q&A Bank

Practitioner Notes

Keep answers process-led, evidence-led, and number-led.

Walk me through a manual IBM ELP.

I scope products and legal entities, collect deployment and entitlement evidence, normalize product/edition/metric, calculate consumption per metric, reconcile against Passport Advantage/PoE entitlements, flag assumptions, and present a RAG executive position backed by evidence.

Why it matters: Whether you understand method, evidence, and executive output.

Why is ILMT mandatory?

IBM grants sub-capacity only when the client can prove eligible virtualized consumption continuously. ILMT is the accepted evidence source. Without healthy ILMT, I calculate full capacity for affected workloads and document the exposure.

Why it matters: Whether you know sub-capacity risk.

How do you validate FNMS IBM output?

I run the FNMS health check, confirm ARL/PURL currency, validate inventory freshness, link purchases to licenses, compare PVU products against ILMT 90-day peaks, and investigate recognition or mapping discrepancies before calling it final.

Why it matters: Whether you treat tools as draft evidence.

What is the IBM Installation Manager trump card?

IBM Installation Manager maintains installed.xml, often under /var/ibm/InstallationManager or C:\ProgramData\IBM\Installation Manager. It records IBM-installed products and is a strong manual discovery source when generic tools miss evidence.

Why it matters: Whether you know practical discovery.

How do you explain findings to executives?

I lead with a traffic-light summary, total exposure, total optimization opportunity, top risks, and a 30/60/90-day roadmap. The detailed workbook remains the evidence appendix.

Why it matters: Whether you can translate technical SAM into decisions.

How do you keep IBM licensing knowledge current?

I keep the position current by validating against the current PVU table, Cloud Pak VPC rules, ILS vs ILMT requirements, FNMS/ILMT behavior, and recent audit-defense patterns.

Why it matters: Whether you combine integrity with confidence.
